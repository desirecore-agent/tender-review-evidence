# Using independent evidence review

Provide the review question, relevant tender/bid/amendment materials and the findings or draft needing independent review. Identify authorized locations and any requested delivery form. Narrow requests are valid; a full six-product export is unnecessary.

Ask, for example: “Check whether these three high-risk findings stand up. Search the amendment and alternate bid appendices for counterevidence. Explain what to retain, withdraw or follow up, and where the evidence is.”

Expect usable per-issue opinions plus scope and limitations. The reviewer may identify new omissions but marks new findings for another specialist's confirmation. Reading or planning alone is incomplete. If evidence is unavailable, expect an explanation of the affected conclusion and a precise request, alongside completed work.

For revised inputs, identify the changes. Ask for affected conclusions to be rechecked. Do not silently reuse an old decision for new documents.

If you explicitly need the legacy v1.2 JSON export, request it separately and see [dependencies](dependencies.md). Export validation does not establish review accuracy. Ordinary narrative review needs only available document/image and calculation capabilities appropriate to the task.

Use authorized materials only. The configured model may process sent content; additional services require authorization. Outputs support human decisions. Existing evaluation is synthetic, not proof of real-tender accuracy.
