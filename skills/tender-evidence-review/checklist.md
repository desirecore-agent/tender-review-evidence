# Outcome quality checklist

Use this to judge the actual opinion, not to impose an output layout.

- Can the Lead see which important findings to retain, withdraw, revise or investigate, and why?
- Did important decisions use independently read tender and bid evidence with workable locations?
- Were amendments, exceptions and alternative response locations considered before alleging nonresponse?
- Was unreadable material distinguished from absence, uncertainty from failure, and visual observation from authenticity?
- Were material calculations recomputed with their stated units, tax basis and rounding?
- Were important omissions and recommendation quality considered, beyond reported findings?
- Does the stated scope match actual work, with unfinished parts and their impact visible?
- Are new findings marked for specialist/independent confirmation instead of self-certified?
- Can each correction request be acted on without guessing what to check?
- Has a usable opinion actually been delivered in the requested or suitable form?

A missing substantive answer calls for targeted follow-up. Different wording or layout is not a defect. Coverage, evidence accuracy, missed-important-issue and false-alarm rates can be evaluated against human-agreed cases; do not invent denominators, thresholds or measured results.
